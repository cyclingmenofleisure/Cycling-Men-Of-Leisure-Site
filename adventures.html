The Open Road, Leisurely

Adventures

Two week-long rides every summer, plus multi-day adventures around the country. Here's where the leisure has taken us.

The Classics

Signature Rides

RAGBRAI · Iowa

The Register's Annual Great Bicycle Ride Across Iowa — the world's oldest and largest bike tour, and the road where Adam and Michael met in 2016. Where it all began.

BRAG · Georgia

The Bike Ride Across Georgia — Atlanta to Savannah, and the scene of the 2022 bourbon-and-cigar moment that gave Cycling Men of Leisure its name.

BRAG Spring Tune-Up · Georgia

BRAG's three-day spring warm-up — a shorter Georgia jaunt to shake off winter and get the legs turning again.

DALMAC · Michigan

Dick Allen's Lansing to Mackinaw — a Michigan institution from the capital to the Straits, finishing in the shadow of the Mighty Mac.

Bourbon Country Burn · Kentucky

A ride through Kentucky's bourbon distilleries. The motto says it all: ride bikes, drink bourbon.

Gorilla Century · Pittsburg, KS

A hundred miles through southeast Kansas, loud jerseys and all — home, per the guys, of "the latest century ride EVER."

ODRAM · Michigan

One Day Ride Across Michigan — coast to coast in a single push, sunrise to sunset.

Elephant Rock · Colorado

A front-range favorite out of Castle Rock — big Colorado views and thin mountain air.

BRAN · Nebraska

The Bike Ride Across Nebraska — wide skies, rolling country, and the occasional character-building headwind.

Holland 100 · Michigan

A hundred-mile loop around Holland, Michigan — tulips, lakeshore, and lake-effect everything.

BRAK · Kansas

The Bike Ride Across Kansas — a straight shot through the Sunflower State's small towns and towering grain elevators.

SAGBRAW · Wisconsin

Sprockets' Annual Great Bike Ride Across Wisconsin — Northwoods charm, kettle-moraine hills, and plenty of cheese.
