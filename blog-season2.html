S2E28 · December 24, 2023

Miles, Memories, and More: A Year in Retrospect of Cycling Adventures and Bourbon Stories

What if you could channel the ease of a leisurely bike ride into your daily life? Adam and Michael take a pedal-powered journey through the past year's adventures — over 1,400 miles and 22 nights, from the Appalachian foothills to the heart of Georgia — sharing the highlights and lowlights of their 2023 season, from memorable jerseys to unexpected travel challenges and a credit card stuck in a gas pump.

Ever camped indoors and gotten a warning from a police officer? They did. From encounters with RAGBRAI's executive director to the camaraderie of Bourbon Country Burn campfires, they revisit their most unforgettable moments, their favorite gadgets (including the game-changing bike radar), and their top bourbon stories and recommendations.

Looking ahead, they celebrate the spirit of adventure and connection that keeps them pedaling — and toast to 2024 with more miles, more stories, and more bourbon.

S2E25 · November 12, 2023

Cycling with Purpose: Sara's Story of Philanthropy and Adventure

Imagine pedaling your bicycle across the heart of America — and every mile you ride helps build homes for those in need. That's exactly what guest Sara accomplished through Bike and Build, a story of how cycling can transcend leisure and become a transformative experience for whole communities.

The show celebrates the highs of Sara's 81-day, cross-country journey (including 16-18 build days) but also navigates the lows — honoring the memory of a fellow rider lost on the road and touching on a recent Michigan case, a sober reminder of advocacy for justice and safety in the cycling community.

Looking ahead, Sara previews her next venture with the Fuller Center Bike Adventure — a ride across four states in the name of philanthropy — and the guys, celebrating Cycling Women of Leisure, share her fundraising link and explore some of the most popular cycling routes in the U.S.

S2E24 · October 29, 2023

Tech on Wheels: A Deep Dive into Cycling Computers, Radars, and some other fun stuff

Ever wondered how you could combine your love for cycling with a chance to contribute to a noble cause? Adam and Michael take you on a journey filled with cycling adventures, tech talk, and a touch of museum trivia — sure to intrigue the cyclist in you and maybe even inspire a few to take up the sport.

The adventure begins with a glimpse of the Louisville Slugger Museum and Factory, a virtual visit to the Mary Todd Lincoln House, and a reflection on Adam's 21-mile hike from the Abraham Lincoln Library to his home.

Then they dig into cycling computers — whether you actually need one and how it could reshape your riding — comparing brands like Garmin, Wahoo, and Hammerhead, debating their strengths and limitations against the convenience of a simple smartphone, plus rear-view radar and other gear.

S2E23 · October 15, 2023

Pedaling Community Bonds: Unveiling the Power of Bike Co-Ops with Luke Box, Kentucky's Cycling Crusader

Ever thought about cycling as a primary means of transport and the vital role community bike shops play in making that happen? The guys sit down with Luke Box, who manages a non-profit community bike co-op in Lexington, Kentucky, with a 12-year history of promoting better access to quality bikes.

Luke takes them through the world of volunteer-based bike co-ops and their role in breaking barriers to bike usage — plus the intriguing sport of bike polo, popular in Lexington's cycling community. He peels back the layers on how a community bike shop actually works: the diverse roles volunteers play, keeping bikes reliable, and initiatives that make cycling inclusive, like the co-op's Women/Fem Night.

S2E20 · September 3, 2023

Whiskey Wisdom and Wandering in Kentucky with Phil Kollin, and the Bourbon County Burn

Pour a glass of your favorite spirit and join the guys for a ride through the bourbon-soaked back roads of Kentucky with Phil Kollin of Phil Talks Whiskey. Phil isn't just a whiskey enthusiast but an industry expert on a quest to explore every highway, back road, and no-lane road in the Bluegrass State — a journey that's produced a treasure trove of stories.

They navigate the Kentucky Bourbon Trail and Bourbon Country Burn under Phil's expert guidance, with each distillery revealing a unique experience. Phil talks about legendary tour guides like Freddie Johnson III, his surprising take on signing whiskey bottles, and — for anyone wondering — whether he's a cyclist.

Phil's path to becoming an industry expert is inspiring: he busts bourbon myths, voices his thoughts on the lack of a universal whiskey certification, and previews his upcoming class at the University of Cincinnati. Whether you're a connoisseur or a novice, this one quenches your thirst for knowledge.

S2E19 · August 20, 2023

Bike Repairs, E-Bike Laws, and Cycling Adventures: An Episode with the Men of Leisure

Gear up for an enlightening ride as the Cycling Men of Leisure navigate the mystifying world of bike repairs, on the hunt for a bike-industry guru to answer their technical queries — and hear how one of them has been stalling on taking his bike into the shop. And what's a cycling adventure without the right shorts? They share the five common mistakes cyclists make with shorts and chamois, dispelling myths about gender-specific shorts, why more padding isn't always better, and the big no-no: wearing underwear underneath.

S2E14 · June 4, 2023

Taking Flight with Your Bike: Tips and Tricks for Air Travel with Your Two-Wheeler

Have you ever wondered how to fly with your beloved bicycle without damaging it or breaking the bank? Adam and Michael share their experiences and tips on preparing and packing your bike for air travel — from soft-sided cases and cardboard boxes to shipping options.

They walk through the whole process of disassembling and protecting your bike: loosening components, marking them, releasing air pressure from the tires, and packing securely with the right tools — such as the Thule RoundTrip Sport hard-sided case — plus ways to either buy air cartridges or skip them entirely.

Along the way they cover airline policies for flying with bikes and clever hacks like using pool noodles for extra frame protection, so your bike arrives safe and ready for the next adventure.

S2E7 · February 26, 2023

To Charter or not to Charter — We talk about Bicycle Charters for large rides like BRAG, RAGBRAI, and others

With RAGBRAI 50 registration booming, charters are the talk of every cycling group — so Adam and Michael dive deep to help you decide whether you actually need one or can get by without.

They break charters into four tiers — comfort, comfort plus, glam camping, and bunkhouses — and walk through what each one gets you: transportation to and from the start, campsite setup, shower trucks, community tents, evening events, tent-and-porter service, and even air-conditioned bunkhouses. The bottom line: a charter is never required (your registration covers basic baggage and camping), but the right level can make a big ride feel a lot more like a vacation.

S2E3 · January 15, 2023

Adam's Noble Steed — What Kind of Bikes we ride

In this episode, Adam and Michael talk about what bicycles they ride, the equipment they use, and the odds and ends bolted to their bikes. They also dream up the bikes they'd buy if money were no object, and share a few favorite road-adventure memories along the way.

Adam introduces his "noble steed" — a 2013 Trek Domane 4.3 with 16,000+ miles, carbon wheels, and the aero bars he swears by even uphill — while Michael rolls on his 2016 Bianchi Intrepida "Italian stallion" in classic Celeste, Shimano 105 and all. They get into saddles, SPD vs. look pedals, power-meter pedals, and why comfort and mindset beat chasing the highest-end components every time.

The dream-bike talk runs from a Canyon Ultimate to the $13,000 Trek Domane SLR with hidden storage and electronic shifting — before they both admit they're happiest on the bikes they've got. They close on favorite rides: a freeing rolling stretch through Kentucky horse country at the Bourbon Country Burn, and riding right alongside Lake Michigan from Petoskey up to Mackinac.

S2E1 · January 1, 2023

The Idea that came out of a Cornfield — Our Cycling Origin Story

This was initially recorded to be the very first show. It describes who we are, how we got started in cycling, and what Cycling Men of Leisure means to us — an attitude that puts the journey and the people you meet ahead of racing from point A to point B.

Adam shares how a health scare and a doctor's blunt advice led him from morbidly obese to a hybrid bike, a hilly metro park, and eventually his first century at RAGBRAI 2016 — the ride where he met Michael, who kept telling him "it's not a race." Michael tells his own story: cycling across Nebraska with his father as "Team Sherpa," turning multi-day rides into cherished family time.

They explain that Cycling Men of Leisure is for men and women alike — for anyone who likes to ride, meet new people, and experience the road — and lay out what listeners can expect: multi-day and state rides, packing tips, charter-service advice, gear talk, and the strange small towns you only find on a bike. And no, it's not a team; it's a like-minded community.
