S5E48 · November 6, 2026

How To Win BIG BRAG 2027, With A Route Guessing Contest

The BRAG team joins the show: Ron, Stephanie, and Franklin from Bicycle Ride Across Georgia sit down with Adam and Michael to talk about Big BRAG 2027, the 47th consecutive edition, set for early June. They cover why riders keep coming back year after year, how the ride works for solo riders, families, teams, and first-timers, and the rest of BRAG's calendar: the winter ride on Georgia's coastal islands, the Spring Tune-Up camping festival, and international tours, with Franklin scouting South Africa.

The big news is a route-guessing contest from BRAG and the podcast. Use the guess form here on the site to pick the start town, each overnight stop, the layover day, and the finish. A correct town on the correct day scores two points, and a correct town on the wrong day scores one. One entry per person, the first time-stamped entry counts, and ties are broken by total miles guessed, including the optional century. Fair warning: 2027 heads into a different corridor than past rides, so it won't be easy.

First place wins a free Big BRAG registration. Second gets a BRAG t-shirt and a Cycling Men of Leisure swag bag, and third takes home a Cycling Men of Leisure t-shirt and swag. Entries close at midnight on December 30, and the route is revealed on December 31. Along the way, Adam keeps fielding invitations to join BRAG's overseas tours.

S5E47 · October 30, 2026

We Build A Smarter Gift List By Mixing Bikes, Travel, And Everyday Comfort

The Listener Spotlight reveal opens the show: the city from the last clues was Rochester, New York, and this time Adam cracked it himself. After St. Louis and New York City both missed, he landed on Rochester by way of the subway clue. Michael walks through the clues: Colonel Nathan Rochester, Susan B. Anthony, flour milling, Kodak, and the Erie Canal aqueduct that made Rochester America's first boom town. The subway ran in the old canal bed from 1927 to 1956.

Along the way, the guys laugh off a listener's comment that they repeat themselves, reflect on nearly 140 episodes and the community around them, and tease a BRAG announcement. Adam reports on San Antonio and the Alamo's surprising downtown setting, and Michael shows off his Halloween yard display, complete with a bicycle and a phantom rider on a 1975 beach cruiser. They also talk football, smart lights, and January golf and podcasting plans around PodFest.

The heart of the episode is the first half of the annual holiday gift guide, 30 picks split across cycling, golf and leisure: chamois cream, Lonely Planet's Epic Bike Rides of America, a handmade cycling cap, a folding wall mount for bikes, a travel seat cushion, gripper slippers, a divot tool and alignment sticks, Glencairn whiskey glasses, a rechargeable hand warmer, a Lego orchid, and more.

Cycling news closes the show, with a voice-controlled Garmin head unit and a pricey limited-edition set of Tour de France tires, followed by a new set of Spotlight clues (including a bonus clue about a lovely lady) and a question for listeners: what's the best gift you've ever received?

S5E46 · October 16, 2026

From High-Speed Trains in Europe, to Haunted Cartoons.

Part two of the Halloween special starts with the Listener Spotlight reveal: the city from the last episode's clues was Liverpool, England. Kenneth Rocher and Matt Robinson both got it, while another listener's submission was credited to Antwerp, and Paul's St. Louis guess missed again. Michael walks through how the clues fit, from the town's origins as a military staging point under King John to its royal street plan, its role as a major port, its 1852 public lending library, and the Beatles.

Then Adam reports back from his 17-day Italy and Mediterranean trip, planned for about three years for six people without a tour company. He flew Detroit to Rome, took high-speed trains to Venice and Ravenna, then cruised from Ravenna to Dubrovnik, Split, Albania, Sicily and the Amalfi Coast near Pompeii. After that came Tuscany (Siena, Pisa and Florence) and three days in Rome for the Pantheon, Colosseum and Vatican. He covers train-station logistics, the Doge's Palace, Ravenna's mosaics, a Dubrovnik wall walk, climbing the Leaning Tower, and the toll that 12 to 14 miles a day took on his white-soled shoes.

Michael recounts meeting Armando for a beer during Armando's Katy Trail ride across Missouri, and the guys debate how long a vacation should be. Then comes a ranking of the original Scooby-Doo villains, with a little Hanna-Barbera history about the reused backgrounds, and a look at the real history of trick-or-treating, from harvest and All Saints' traditions to the 1927 first printed use and the rise of trunk-or-treats.

Adam closes with a work conference and a 32-mile ride on a bike that had been sitting too long. A new set of Spotlight clues lands at the end (a revolutionary soldier, money inspired by a resident, flour, a smile, boom-town water and a missed subway), along with a BRAG tease and a call for leisure gift ideas for the annual holiday episodes.
