Est. on a road in Iowa

The Origin Story

2016 · RAGBRAI, Iowa

Two riders, one road

Adam and Michael meet in the middle of Iowa during RAGBRAI. Miles pass discussing cycling, investing, and telling stories. A friendship — and a philosophy — takes root.

2018 · The Return

Back to Iowa

They return to RAGBRAI, and the riding takes on a life of its own. Every summer since: two week-long rides and several multi-day adventures around the country.

2022 · BRAG, Georgia

The bourbon & cigar moment

Enjoying a bourbon and a cigar after a day on the Bike Ride Across Georgia, a passing comment becomes the catalyst. A style is named.

Two months later

CMOL is born

A Facebook page, a website, and a podcast that exemplify the style, the cycling ideology, and the life philosophy. Cycling Men of Leisure — it's for women too — is official.

Membership of the Spirit

Are You a Cycling Man (or Woman) of Leisure?

I.

You savor the after-ride

A bourbon, a beer, or your favorite libation — possibly a cigar — enjoyed properly once the wheels stop turning.

II.

You collect stories, not strava crowns

You enjoy sharing your love of cycling, meeting new people, and swapping tales with those who ride like you think.

III.

Everyone's welcome

Devoted roadie or only semi-interested and looking for a new perspective — CMOL welcomes all, including the ladies.
