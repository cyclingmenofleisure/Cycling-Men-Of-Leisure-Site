What We've Been Riding

DaBrim Cycling Helmet Brim

★★★★★  5 out of 5

Pros

  • Excellent sun protection for your face, ears, and neck
  • Keeps rain and glare out of your eyes
  • Clips onto just about any helmet — on or off in seconds
  • Lightweight and packs down for travel
  • A signature leisure look once you embrace it

Cons

  • Catches crosswinds — keep a hand on it during gusty descents
  • Takes a ride or two (and some good-natured ribbing) to get used to

Check it out →

Cycplus Electric Air Pump A2 Pro

★★★★★  4 out of 5

Pros

  • Great product, well constructed
  • Desired pressure can be pre-programmed
  • Doubles as a charger
  • Plenty of juice to air up several tires without daily charging
  • Great price point for the quality

Cons

  • Very loud when in use
  • Be careful not to pull out the valve core when unscrewing the unit from the tire
