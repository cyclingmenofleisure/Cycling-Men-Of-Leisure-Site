The Strava Club

Log your miles, cheer on your fellow leisure riders, and be part of the community between episodes. Whether you're chasing a century or just spinning around the block, there's a place for you in the Cycling Men of Leisure Strava Club.

Better Together

Why Join the Club

Log Your Miles

Every ride counts. Track your rides alongside the whole leisure community and watch the club's miles add up, week after week.

See Everyone's Rides

Follow along as fellow members roll out across the country — from RAGBRAI training to a quiet evening loop — and give a kudos where it's earned.

Friendly Competition

A little leaderboard never hurt anyone. Chase segments, join challenges, and enjoy the camaraderie that keeps the pedals turning.

Three Easy Steps

How to Join

1 · Get on Strava

Download the free Strava app or sign up at strava.com — it works with just about any bike computer, watch, or phone.

2 · Find the Club

Search for "Cycling Men of Leisure" or use the button below to head straight to our club page.

3 · Request to Join

Tap "Join" and you're in. Start logging rides and say hello to the leisure crew.
